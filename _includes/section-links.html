    (each nav styles its own list) Each entry decides for itself whether it is the current section, using the `match` rule in the data file: `prefix` also marks child pages, so reading an individual story still marks "stories". The urls in _data/sections.yml are already canonical, with their trailing slash, so this compares them to page.url DIRECTLY.
